Our approach

Every detailed guide should explain the steps clearly and identify its source wherever possible — such as a named granth, puran, published paddhati or regional tradition.

This site is a learning and reference resource, not a claim that every Hindu tradition follows one identical procedure. For complex rites, consult a qualified priest from your tradition.
