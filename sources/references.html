Editorial principles

Sources & References स्रोत एवं संदर्भ

A transparent method for documenting the source, edition and tradition behind each guide.

Our source standard

For each ritual guide, record the name of the text or paddhati, author or editor where applicable, publisher or stable URL, edition, chapter/section/page, language, and date reviewed.

Distinguish source types

  • Primary text: a named granth, puran, scripture or traditional text consulted directly.
  • Published paddhati: a ritual manual or edition used for the practical sequence.
  • Regional or family custom: clearly identified as a local practice rather than a universal rule.
  • Editorial explanation: plain-language guidance written for this website.

Review status

Use labels such as “Draft”, “Source identified”, “Reviewed against source” and “Needs expert review”. Do not label a page “authentic” solely because a source title is mentioned.

Suggested source record

Guide: [Puja name]
Text / paddhati: [Exact title]
Author / editor: [If known]
Publisher / URL: [Details]
Chapter / section / page: [Location]
Tradition / regional notes: [Notes]
Reviewed by / date: [Details]
Status: Draft / Reviewed / Needs expert review

This website is a general reference and does not replace instruction from a qualified practitioner in a specific tradition.
